Profile

I make design
prove itself.

I'm a senior product designer with 15+ years turning ambiguous problems into shippable, measurable experiences. I work where research, business goals, and data meet — and I'm happiest when a design decision can be tested and defended.

Lately I design enterprise commerce flows at Cisco, where my work supports a monthly revenue stream and I use A/B testing to move conversion. Before that I shaped IoT acquisition at Verizon, B2B and subscription commerce for Coca-Cola and Match, and design systems and brand work across SaaS and agency teams. I'm increasingly curious about agent-driven interfaces and what good UX looks like when an AI is a participant in the flow.

A note on numbers: figures like $10M+ monthly revenue and a 30% conversion lift reflect the Cisco Commerce business I contribute to — not any single project on this site. Where a project's outcome isn't measured yet, I say so and show how I'd measure it.

How I work

Accountable by default

Every design should have a job and a way to know if it did it. I frame the metric before I frame the screen.

Data-informed, not data-led

Research and analytics point the way; judgment and craft still make the call.

Test and learn

I'd rather ship a small, instrumented bet and learn than argue a big one in a deck.

Honest about the record

I only claim what the work supports. Gaps get labeled, not invented.
