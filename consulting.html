Good to know

Questions, answered

Who owns the final website and accounts?
You do. You own your domain, hosting, and third-party accounts, and you keep the delivered files. Ownership never depends on staying on a support plan.
What costs are separate from the project fee?
Hosting, domain registration, paid fonts or stock assets, software subscriptions, ad spend, and any store/platform fees. These are disclosed and approved before anything is purchased.
How long do projects take?
Indicative windows: a launch page in 2–3 weeks, a small site in 4–6 weeks, a concept sprint in 2–4 weeks — from kickoff and receipt of the materials I need. Dates are confirmed against actual capacity before we book.
How do revisions work?
Each project includes two consolidated revision rounds at agreed milestones. Larger changes or new scope are quoted separately so the timeline and price stay honest.
What's the difference between a prototype and a production app?
A concept sprint delivers a clickable prototype and a plan — great for validating an idea or showing investors. A production app (real backend, store release) is a separate, larger engagement that starts with paid discovery.
How does ongoing support work?
Optional monthly plans reserve a fixed block of time for improvements. One active request at a time, a visible queue, and a two-business-day acknowledgment. You can cancel before the next renewal.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Monthly plans are cancellable before the next billing date, and projects have clear booking/handoff milestones. I'll always tell you your next billing date and how offboarding works.

Also available as separate, staged services once a site is live: basic SEO is included in every build; focused SEO, paid-media, and social-content pilots can be scoped individually — no performance or ranking guarantees.
