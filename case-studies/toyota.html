Project overview

A collaboration with KB Studios on Toyota's long-running "Let's Go Places" platform, built around the launch of the new RAV4 Hybrid. The work was brand and visual design in service of a national campaign.

The problem

A vehicle launch inside a mature brand platform has to do two things at once: honor the established "Let's Go Places" identity and still make the RAV4 Hybrid feel new and distinct. The creative challenge was fitting the product into a beloved brand story without disappearing into it.

Audience & approach

Primary audience is prospective RAV4 Hybrid buyers within Toyota's broad campaign reach. Working alongside KB Studios, I helped craft creative that stayed true to the campaign platform while spotlighting the hybrid's story.

Design process

Visual / brand design contributions to the campaign in collaboration with the KB Studios team. Specific deliverables and assets are available on request.

Outcome & impact — how I'd measure it

For a brand campaign, impact lives in reach and recall:

  • Campaign reach and engagement across placements.
  • Brand/campaign recall and alignment to "Let's Go Places."
  • Consideration and lead signals for the RAV4 Hybrid.

Reflections

Working within an iconic brand platform is a discipline in collaboration and consistency — contributing to something larger than any single asset while still making the new product sing.
