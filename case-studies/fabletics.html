Project overview

A social-media product campaign for the activewear brand Fabletics. The work was visual and brand-driven: creative built to showcase product and hold attention across social placements.

The problem

Social creative has seconds to work. It has to be unmistakably on-brand, showcase the product, and earn a stop in a crowded feed — all within tight platform formats. The challenge was consistency and impact at the pace social demands.

Audience & approach

Primary audience is Fabletics' social followers and prospective customers in-feed. I designed creative that stayed true to the brand system while optimizing for the attention economics of social — strong composition, clear product focus, and format-appropriate layouts.

Design process

Campaign visual design across social placements, aligned to brand guidelines. Specific assets are available on request.

Outcome & impact — how I'd measure it

This was brand/campaign work, so impact lives in engagement and conversion signals I'd track:

  • Engagement rate and stop-rate on the creative.
  • Click-through and conversion from campaign placements.
  • Brand consistency across the asset set.

Reflections

Brand and campaign work keeps my craft sharp — composition, hierarchy, and restraint — and reminds me that even the most data-driven product still has to earn attention.
