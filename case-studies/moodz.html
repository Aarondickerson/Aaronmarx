TL;DR
  • Moodz is a video-menu platform dedicated to creating a visual experience.
  • It reimagines the menu as motion — showing food and drink in video rather than static text and photos.
  • I designed the product / UI experience for browsing and choosing from a video-first menu.
  • A video-forward menu concept; outcome metrics would come from testing whether video aids the dining decision.

Project overview

Moodz is a platform that turns menus into video. Instead of reading descriptions and scanning static photos, diners watch short clips that convey the dish, the drink, and the mood. My work focused on the product and UI design of that video-first browsing experience.

The problem

Static menus undersell food. Text and the occasional photo rarely capture what makes a dish appealing, and that gap costs restaurants orders and upsell. The design challenge: build a browsing experience where video is the primary content, yet the menu stays fast to scan, easy to order from, and comfortable on a phone — video that informs appetite without getting in the way of the decision.

Users & research

Primary users are diners browsing a menu; the platform also serves the restaurants publishing it. I'm describing the user needs I designed toward: helping diners decide quickly and helping restaurants showcase their dishes.

Strategy & approach

Make video the hero without sacrificing the fundamentals of a menu: scan-ability, clear pricing, and a frictionless path to choosing. Let motion do the persuading while the interface keeps ordering simple.

Design process

UI for a video-forward menu — browsing, item detail, and the transition from watching to choosing. Specific screens are available on request.

Validation & iteration

For a video menu, I'd validate on whether video actually aids the decision or slows it, and whether it drives selection of featured items.

Outcome & impact — how I'd measure it

  • Menu engagement: items viewed, video completion vs. scroll-past.
  • Selection rate for video-featured items.
  • Time-to-decision — does video help or hinder.
  • Restaurant-side adoption of video content.

What I'd do next · Reflections

Moodz was an exercise in letting rich media lead without drowning the task underneath it — a balance that shows up anywhere motion meets a transactional flow.
